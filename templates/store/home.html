Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about ordering, availability, and fulfillment.

How do I place an order?
Browse the catalog, add items with live availability to your cart, and checkout—no calls or manual confirmations needed. If checkout succeeds, your order is locked in.
Are stock and prices real-time?
Yes. The catalog reflects live stock levels and current pricing. If an item is visible and in stock, you can order it.
Do I need an account before checkout?
Creating a business account speeds up repeat orders and documents, but you can start browsing immediately and complete checkout when you’re ready.
What happens after I place an order?
We process it right away. You’ll get status updates, and dispatch starts promptly according to your chosen delivery option.
Can I modify or cancel an order?
Orders move fast. If you need changes, contact us immediately after checkout. We’ll do our best before dispatch begins.
What’s the minimum order and shipping scope?
Minimums and delivery coverage depend on destination and product type. You’ll see available options at checkout.
How do you handle invoices and taxes?
Invoices are generated automatically at checkout with your company details. Applicable VAT and duties are shown before you pay.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We use encrypted transport, restricted access, and strict retention practices. Business profiles and payment data are protected.
